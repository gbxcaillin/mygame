Friend Bot

A stand-in opponent for testing online play on your own. It joins a game you host and plays with the real AI — so you can try the collection draft, the wager, the dice and the card transfer without a second device.

  1. In the game: Opponent → Friend (online) → Host a game. Note the 4-letter code.
  2. Enter that code below, pick a difficulty, choose whether the bot wagers, and Connect.
  3. Back in the game, finish your own draft (pick 5) — the match starts and the bot plays.
Host a game, then enter the code.
Note: the bot only wagers if you also tick Wager on your side — both must agree. If the bot loses a wager it forfeits one of its cards to you; if it wins, it takes one of yours. It has no collection of its own, so its losses just feed your collection for testing.
